Accounting System

OMMAS is a unified web platform for PMGSY rural roads. It consolidates project execution and accounting data into one dashboard. Field inputs from mobile apps (for surveys and quality checks) feed geo-tagged data into the system in real time.

Real-time dashboards and GIS maps let officials track every project stage from planning through construction and maintenance, ensuring efficient, transparent oversight.

About PMGSY

Pradhan Mantri Gram Sadak Yojana

The Pradhan Mantri Gram Sadak Yojana (PMGSY), was launched by the Govt. of India to provide connectivity to unconnected Habitations as part of a poverty reduction strategy. Govt. of India is endeavoring to set high and uniform technical and management standards and facilitating policy development and planning at State level in order to ensure sustainable management of the rural roads network.

According to latest figures made available by the State Governments under a survey to identify Core Network as part of the PMGSY programme, about 1.67 lakh Unconnected Habitations are eligible for coverage under the programme. This involves construction of about 3.71 lakh km. of roads for New Connectivity and 3.68 lakh km. under upgradation.

The PMGSY Journey

  1. 2000

    PMGSY I

    The first phase of PMGSY was launched to provide all-weather road connectivity to eligible unconnected rural habitations across the country.

  2. 2013

    PMGSY II

    PMGSY Phase II focused on upgrading existing rural roads to improve connectivity and support growing transportation needs.

  3. 2016

    RCPLWEA

    The Road Connectivity Project for LWE-affected Areas (RCPLWEA) was launched to improve road connectivity in 44 LWE-affected districts across 9 states.

  4. 2019

    PMGSY III

    PMGSY Phase III was launched to upgrade 1.25 lakh km of roads, improving habitations connectivity to markets, schools, hospitals, and other essential services.

  5. 2023

    PM-JANMAN & VVP

    Road connectivity was expanded under PM-JANMAN and the Vibrant Villages Programme (VVP) to improve roads for remote tribal and border communities.

  6. 2024

    PMGSY IV (current phase)

    PMGSY Phase IV was approved to connect 25,000 unconnected habitations identified under the 2011 Census by constructing around 62,500 km of all-weather roads.

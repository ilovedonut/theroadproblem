# Andhra city boundary follow-up

Checked 16 October 2026. Official PDFs below were retrieved; the APSEC reservation schedules were rendered locally and visually inspected because their tables were absent from text extraction. Counts are verified current electoral-roster evidence, not certification of current polygon geometry.

## Vijayawada

- **86 wards confirmed by primary official source.** AP State Election Commission hosts [Vijayawada reservation notification](https://sec.ap.gov.in/Uploads/Doc26/Res/MC_Vijayawada.pdf). Page 1: Municipal Administration Department, Roc. No. M-1-115516/2026, dated 13.10.2026, NTR District Gazette No. 97. Page 2 schedule explicitly lists Vijayawada Municipal Corporation and **86** in the number-of-wards column, signed by District Election Authority/Collector, NTR district.
- This is a ward reservation notification. It confirms the current ward total; it does **not** contain ward boundary descriptions or the current circle-to-ward mapping.
- Legacy 64-ward material must therefore remain a historical scheme. Three-circle administration is a useful discovery lead, but the final 86-ward-to-circle allocation was not verified from a current primary order in this bounded pass. Do not certify a 28/29/29 allocation solely from a May 2026 draft or press report.
- Acquisition gate: obtain final September 2026 delimitation notification and all corrections through VMC, C&DMA/AP Gazette, then current circle roster/vector map. Reconcile all 86 local ward identities with the APSEC schedule before publishing polygons. No supported current official vector download was verified here.

## Visakhapatnam

- **120 wards confirmed by primary official source.** APSEC hosts [Greater Visakhapatnam reservation notification](https://sec.ap.gov.in/Uploads/Doc26/Res/MC_Greater%20Visakhpatnam.pdf). Page 1 schedule explicitly says Greater Visakhapatnam Municipal Corporation and **120 Wards**. Page 2 is signed by Collector/District Magistrate, Visakhapatnam, on **13-10-2026**. Header: GVMC-EST3/181/2026-JA(D4) C&D-GVMC, I/467076/2026.
- **Ten zones have an official approved source**, not merely a proposal title. [Government memo and zone map hosted by GVMC](https://www.gvmc.gov.in/wss/image_uploads/city%20town%20planning.pdf), Memo No.2985738/G/2025, dated **24 September 2025**, explicitly approves rationalisation into ten zones. It includes an internal October 2025 instruction to publish the memo/map for day-to-day administration.
- Those ten approved zone names: Bheemunipatnam, Madhurawada, East, North, South, West, Pendurthi, Gajuwaka, Aganampudi and Anakapalli.
- **Version trap:** that approved memo's membership table uses the old **98-ward** scheme. Preserve it as the 2025 zone/ward relationship; do not map its ward numbers onto the current 120 wards. A current 120-ward-to-zone crosswalk still needs final primary verification.
- The [GVMC town-planning landing page](https://gvmc.gov.in/thirdlevel.htm?OtpCode=2026&levelid=200-111) mixes a proposed 120-ward map, preliminary notification, approved zone memo and legacy 98-ward gazettes. Therefore its headings alone are insufficient to establish a current complete layer.
- [120-ward preliminary notification](https://www.gvmc.gov.in/WhatsNew/Whats_New_DocMay_909.pdf): Rc.No.00165/2026/TP Section/G1/G2, dated **17 May 2026**, explicitly labelled **PRELIMINARY NOTIFICATION**, inviting objections within seven days. Do not promote this to final boundaries.
- [Proposed 120-ward map](https://www.gvmc.gov.in/wss/image_uploads/prop120wrds.pdf) explicitly labels proposed wards, proposed zonal boundaries and proposed ward boundaries. It shows ten named zones but a different zone ordering/membership from the approved 98-ward source. This remains draft lineage until reconciled with the final delimitation notification, corrections and current administrative order.
- Acquisition gate: obtain final 2026 ward delimitation gazette and current zone crosswalk from GVMC/C&DMA, vector geometry if available, and reconcile 120 unique ward identities. The old 98-ward gazette is historical; a current reservation count does not make a draft map final.

# Southern city jurisdiction research

Research checked 16 October 2026. This is a planning inventory, not a certified boundary release. Primary official sources only support the factual claims below. A search engine crawl date is not the legal effective date of a boundary. Municipal wards, revenue districts, master-plan zones, health circles and engineering maintenance divisions must be separate boundary schemes.

## Bengaluru

- Current city navigation must accommodate Greater Bengaluru Authority (GBA) and five distinct city corporations: Central, North, South, East and West. The official GBA site and official map-download page list those corporations. Do not build a new navigation system around the old single BBMP/eight-zone hierarchy.
- Sources: [GBA](https://gba.karnataka.gov.in/), [official corporation map downloads](https://www.bbmp.gov.in/maps/), [official location-to-corporation/ward map](https://gba.karnataka.gov.in/KnowYourNewCorporation/index.html), [Central corporation](https://bengalurucentral.karnataka.gov.in/), [North corporation](https://bengalurunorth.karnataka.gov.in/).
- Central and North official homepages each identify ZONE-1 and ZONE-2 officers. This establishes sub-corporation zonal administration; it does not by itself certify named zone polygons or membership of every ward. Obtain current dated zone orders/rosters for all five corporations before publishing that intermediate level.
- Ward counts are deliberately not certified in this note. Contemporary discovery results conflict between 368 and 369 and between 111 and 112 for West, while older material also describes 198, 225 and 243 schemes. Do not hardcode any total until the final notification plus all subsequent corrigenda and the official map files have been archived and reconciled. Official maps endpoints were intermittently inaccessible during this research.
- The official map-download landing page lists each corporation separately. A searchable official PDF at `https://bbmp.gov.in/gba-corporations-delimitation-2025/assets/BENGALURU%20SOUTH%20CITY%20CORPORATION_176%20-%20Bangalore%20South.pdf` bears notification UDD 293 BBS 2025, dated 30 September 2025. It is a useful source-discovery lead, but its date is insufficient to establish it as the final current revision. Archive later final notifications/corrections before use.
- Acquisition: capture notification, effective date, corporation/zone/ward IDs, source checksum, original maps and public GIS geometry where officially obtainable. If only PDFs are available, request vector data from GBA; reviewed digitisation is a fallback, labelled derived and linked to the original. Ward numbers are scoped to the corporation and boundary version; never use a bare ward number as a global identity.
- Road responsibility: the GBA service directory exposes Road History 2.0, GIS Viewer, tenders and grievances. Use actual asset registers, contracts and transfer orders to identify responsible city corporation/department and any other road-owning agency. Geometric inclusion in a corporation establishes location, not road ownership. Keep the existing Bengaluru Urban district road-import footprint separate from the GBA/corporation footprint.

## Hyderabad

- Current government sources confirm three municipal corporations: Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation (GHMC), Cyberabad Municipal Corporation (CMC) and Malkajgiri Municipal Corporation (MMC). The official Telangana press release of 22 July 2026 discusses works across all three, and HYDRAA's official current jurisdiction page lists all three. Thus neither the old GHMC 6-zone/30-circle/150-ward system nor a single citywide GHMC node should be presented as the complete current hierarchy.
- Sources: [Telangana July 2026 press release](https://www.telangana.gov.in/news/press-releases/2026/07/%E0%B0%B9%E0%B1%88%E0%B0%A6%E0%B0%B0%E0%B0%BE%E0%B0%AC%E0%B0%BE%E0%B0%A6%E0%B1%8D-%E0%B0%AE%E0%B0%B2%E0%B1%8D%E0%B0%95%E0%B0%BE%E0%B0%9C%E0%B0%BF%E0%B0%97%E0%B0%BF%E0%B0%B0%E0%B0%BF-%E0%B0%B8/), [HYDRAA jurisdiction](https://hydraa.telangana.gov.in/about), [GHMC](https://www.ghmc.gov.in/ghmc.aspx), [GHMC contacts](https://ghmc.gov.in/KeyContacts.aspx).
- Warning: the GHMC homepage simultaneously links a 25 December 2025 notification for 300 wards and repeats a 6/30/150 description. The [official February 2026 RTI PDF](https://www.ghmc.gov.in/Documents/09022026151924RTI_2026.pdf) also combines legacy introduction/maps with newer ward material. Publication timestamp alone does not make every table current.
- Acquisition: archive the December 2025 delimitation/zone-circle orders, February 2026 three-corporation constitution notification and subsequent corrections. Require an explicit corporation-to-zone-to-circle-to-ward mapping from their operative annexures. Contemporary 12/60/300 totals are discovery leads, not independently certified here as the final October 2026 allocation. Avoid announcing exact split counts until the current official annexures are reconciled.
- TGRAC exposes a [ward feature service](https://tgrac.telangana.gov.in/arcgis/rest/services/GHMCHealth_Folder/GHMC_Health_Facilities/MapServer/2). Its existence does not establish a current boundary version. Compare IDs/counts/geometry with the legal release; otherwise retain it as historical/unverified.
- Road-management layers: city corporation engineering departments plus explicitly documented development/maintenance agencies. HMDA's [official portal](https://www.hmda.gov.in/) separately describes Hyderabad Metropolitan Region, expanded by G.O.Ms.No.68 of 12 March 2025. This metropolitan planning region is not equivalent to the municipal-city footprint. HMDA's [official ORR material](https://dev.hmda.gov.in/maps/) identifies HGCL's ORR development/maintenance/management role; do not label all roads within HMDA as HMDA-owned. Record current road-level contracts/handovers, rather than treating traffic-police or HYDRAA boundaries as road-maintenance ownership.

## Chennai

- Official GCC council page: three regions (North, Central, South), 15 zones and 200 divisions/wards. These are a practical municipal navigation hierarchy; revenue districts/taluks and the Chennai Metropolitan Area belong in separate schemes.
- [Council structure](https://chennaicorporation.gov.in/gcc/council/about-council/); [delimited ward portal](https://www.chennaicorporation.gov.in/gcc/delimited_ward/).
- Best acquisition path in this group: official [GCC ArcGIS administrative boundary service](https://gisgcc.chennaicorporation.gov.in/server/rest/services/GCCPublic/GCC_AdminBoundary/MapServer), with region layer 6, [zone layer 5](https://gisgcc.chennaicorporation.gov.in/server/rest/services/GCCPublic/GCC_AdminBoundary/MapServer/5) and [ward layer 4](https://gisgcc.chennaicorporation.gov.in/server/rest/services/GCCPublic/GCC_AdminBoundary/MapServer/4). Polygon service supports JSON/GeoJSON/PBF and pagination; native CRS is EPSG:32644. Export with `outSR=4326`, preserve original object IDs/ward/zone fields, validate expected counts and map ward membership by spatial/legal cross-check. Confirm permission/terms before republishing; public accessibility does not by itself establish a reuse licence.
- The ward portal explicitly says gazette notifications prevail if discrepancies occur. Its linked January 17, 2022 [Gazette](https://www.chennaicorporation.gov.in/gcc/delimited_ward/pdf/46_Ex_VI_2.pdf-for-Author.pdf) is a reservation notification confirming 200 wards, not a replacement source for boundary coordinates. A prominently searchable 2018 `delimitation_draft` PDF remains draft material and must not be mistaken for final geometry.
- Zones as labelled on GCC's mapping: I Thiruvotriyur, II Manali, III Madhavaram, IV Tondiarpet, V Royapuram, VI Thiru-Vi-Ka Nagar, VII Ambattur, VIII Anna Nagar, IX Teynampet, X Kodambakkam, XI Valasaravakkam, XII Alandur, XIII Adyar, XIV Perungudi, XV Sholinganallur. Validate names/IDs against exported current service and operative gazette before seeding.
- [GCC roads department](https://chennaicorporation.gov.in/gcc/department/road/) distinguishes Bus Route Roads from interior roads and road-cut permissions. Keep that operational classification in the road inventory; do not infer highway ownership from zone inclusion. Import relevant Tamil Nadu Highways/NHAI contracts and handover records as additional agency evidence.

## Coimbatore

- Official zone map: North, East, South, West and Central, 20 divisions per zone, 100 total. [Official zone map PDF](https://ccmc.gov.in/img/upload/Zone%20Map.pdf).
- [Official delimitation landing page](https://ccmc.gov.in/index.php/createpage/delimitation-of-ward) provides a ward finder, delimitation map, Format 2D and [2018 delimitation gazette](https://ccmc.gov.in/img/upload/Delimitation%202018%20Gazette.pdf). Acquire originals and exact release metadata; no clean downloadable current vector endpoint was verified during this research. Request GIS export from CCMC, or digitise the legal maps with review and a derived-data label.
- [Engineering page](https://ccmc.gov.in/index.php/services/Engineering) describes municipal road maintenance but also retains outdated four-zone staffing text despite the official five-zone map. Use current zone map/roster for the hierarchy rather than copying department prose.
- [Citizens charter](https://ccmc.gov.in/img/upload/CitizensCharterEnglish1.pdf) distinguishes roads belonging to the corporation from national/state highways within the city. It is historical evidence of the distinction, not current lengths or current agency assignments. Validate actual asset ownership/engineering division from recent registers and contracts. Municipal extent, Coimbatore district and planning-area extent are separate geometries.

## Kochi

- Current official Kerala SEC ward list has 76 numbered wards, codes C07003001 through C07003076. [SEC ward roster](https://www.sec.kerala.gov.in/public/cnstncy/wd/d0f41644-754b-4b6e-a8dc-97ecbf5fee16). [Corporation councillor list for 2025–2030](https://kochicorporation.lsgkerala.gov.in/index.php/ml/list-councilors-2025-2030/647), published February 9, 2026, is a second current identity source. Do not use the old 74-ward roster.
- Official [phone/office directory](https://kochicorporation.lsgkerala.gov.in/ml/phone-numbers/404) lists six zonal offices: Vyttila, Edapally, Fort Kochi, Mattanchery, Palluruthy and Pachalam. Treat these as operational office areas; obtain current explicit ward membership and geometry before rendering six official zone polygons. Do not use another source's historical Vaduthala listing in place of the current directory's Pachalam without reconciliation.
- Corporation's [delimitation notice](https://kochicorporation.lsgkerala.gov.in/en/delimitation-commission-kerala-notification/590), dated November 19, 2024, links a [KSMART map](https://wardmap.ksmart.live/files/Ernakulam_Cochin_01JBBD7151DY27T8X2GJ2ZD40P.html). This is a discovery path, not proof the 2024 map is the final post-2025 revision. Obtain final Kerala Delimitation Commission notification/corrections; compare current SEC IDs and 76-count. Ask Information Kerala Mission/LSGD for versioned vector export if no supported public GIS download is provided.
- Road agencies to represent through evidence: municipal engineering; Kerala PWD; national-highway authority; project-specific GCDA and CSML delivery. [GCDA official road-development page](https://gcda.kerala.gov.in/mal/public/projects/road-development) lists roads it formed; [CSML road procurement](https://csml.co.in/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/RFP-Roads-West-Kochi-4.pdf) demonstrates project-delivery role. Construction by an agency in 2020 does not prove it remains today's maintenance owner. Separate those relationships and dates.
- Kochi municipality, Ernakulam district and greater Kochi planning area must remain separate.

## Thiruvananthapuram

- Official SEC corporation list gives **101 wards**, local body C01001: [SEC list](https://www.sec.kerala.gov.in/public/ps/lb/da39a3ee5e6b4b0d3255bfef95601890af14/U/C). Avoid the legacy 100-ward assumption.
- Official [zonal office directory](https://tmc.lsgkerala.gov.in/en/corporation-zonal-offices) lists 11 offices with ward-name groupings: Attipra, Ulloor, Kadakampalli, Fort, Thiruvallam, Nemom, Vattiyoorkavu, Kudappanakunnu, Sreekaryam, Kazhakuttom and Vizhinjam. The [organisation page](https://tmc.lsgkerala.gov.in/ml/organisational-structure) still says six, so the directory and final 101-ward roster require a dated reconciliation before formal polygon publication. Some wards may be served through head office; do not force all into 11 groups without evidence.
- [Municipal delimitation page](https://tmc.lsgkerala.gov.in/ml/publication-kerala-delimitation-commission/1646) contains November 2024 draft, hearing and errata files. Its draft label is explicit. Use this only to discover source lineage; acquire final 2025 notification and subsequent corrections, and use current SEC roster as the identity check. KSMART/IKM/LSGD export request or reviewed legal-map digitisation is the boundary acquisition path; a supported current vector endpoint was not verified.
- [Municipal administrative/engineering responsibilities](https://tmc.lsgkerala.gov.in/en/page/106) include road construction/maintenance. Kerala PWD maintains a separate Roads Maintenance office structure; KRFB's [official projects page](https://krfb.org/eng/ongoing-projects/) describes TCRIP. Keep corporation/PWD/KRFB responsibility and project delivery separate and resolve each road from current contracts, assets and handovers. Corporation zone is an administrative address, not automatic ownership.

## Cross-city release gates suggested by this research

1. Every city-selector shape identifies its intended footprint: municipal, multi-corporation city or planning/metropolitan envelope. Avoid drawing a district and labelling it a city.
2. Current legal release and any corrigenda archived, checksummed and dated; draft and historical schemes retained separately.
3. Ward/circle/zone hierarchy reconciles exact official codes and counts, without relying only on website prose or scrape freshness.
4. Vector geometry is valid, CRS-labelled, inside the correct parent except documented exceptions, and visibly reviewed against official maps. Licensing and attribution tracked.
5. Engineering divisions and agency responsibilities form separate assignments, with evidence, effective dates, type of duty and confidence. Spatial membership never silently becomes road ownership.
6. When current administrative polygons cannot be verified, ship the verified city selector plus a clearly stated unavailable sublayer; do not fabricate zones or borrow old boundaries.

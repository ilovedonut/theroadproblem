# North, east and central city jurisdiction research

Research date: 16 October 2026. This is a research inventory and proposed onboarding model, not a certified GIS release. No application code or database has been changed.

Use final effective government orders before older portal text. An electoral ward, municipal administrative zone, development-plan zone, revenue district and road-maintenance division are separate datasets. A city selector can contain several public bodies. Counts below are published evidence, not a substitute for obtaining the actual polygons and their effective dates.

## App design implications

1. Store separate boundary schemes with type, owner, source, effective_from/effective_to, publication date, checksum, licence and validation status. Reusing an ordinal like 'Zone 1' across cities or boundary versions does not establish identity.
2. Link road spans to areas many-to-many by measured intersection. Do not force every road into one ward or infer ownership from its location. Preserve current and historical memberships.
3. At national zoom show labelled city discovery shapes with coverage state. At city zoom show the selected city's default municipal or authority scheme; allow switching to road-maintenance units and wards. The chosen scheme's label must explicitly say 'Municipal wards', 'FMDA zones', 'Engineering divisions', etc.
4. City boundary, road-index coverage and official custodianship each get independent status. An unavailable current ward scheme must not block neutral city navigation, but must prevent claims of ward-based completeness or accountability.
5. Gate launch on current final orders, permission to reuse, geometry validity and topology, completeness of codes and crosswalks, and sample road/authority hand-over checks. A successful portal fetch is not sufficient validation.

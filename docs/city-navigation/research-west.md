# Western city navigation and administrative boundaries

Research checked 16 October 2026. This is a source inventory and acquisition plan, not an assertion that polygon downloads have already been imported or licensed. No app or database changes were made.

## Main modelling rule

Keep city/ULB navigation, revenue districts, municipal administrative zones/ward offices, electoral wards/prabhags, development-plan zones, and road-management responsibilities as distinct versioned layers. Their names overlap but their boundaries and purposes do not. Source publication date, legal effective date and retrieval date must be separate fields. An accessible map viewer or PDF is not an immediately usable vector dataset, nor evidence of unrestricted republication rights.

## Road responsibility: separate from these polygons

These are candidate authority registries to investigate, not assignments that should be inferred from a point falling inside a ward.

- Mumbai: BMC road department and ward offices, plus project-specific MMRDA, MSRDC, state PWD, NHAI, port, airport and MIDC records where applicable. [MMRDA engineering overview](https://www.mmrda.maharashtra.gov.in/en/divisions/engineering/overview) documents regional road projects within and outside BMC. Current maintenance requires a dated handover or asset register.
- Pune/PCMC: maintain two ULBs, with PMRDA regional projects separately. [PMRDA Engineering-1](https://www.pmrda.gov.in/en/engineering-1/) explicitly covers ring roads, taluka roads and infrastructure projects. Its [airport connectivity list](https://www.pmrda.gov.in/en/airport/) distinguishes routes implemented through NHAI and PWD. Cantonment and MIDC roads require their own records.
- Ahmedabad: AMC municipal roads and AUDA project or town-planning scheme infrastructure. [AUDA's official role](https://www.auda.org.in/Content.aspx?Id=42&Type=C) includes road infrastructure. State R&B and NHAI records supplement identified corridors. AUDA planning areas are separate from AMC municipal wards.
- Surat: SMC zone engineering and central Road Development, Bridge and Traffic departments. The [official 2026 organogram](https://www.suratmunicipal.gov.in/Content/Documents/Corporation/organogram.pdf?ver=4734) distinguishes zone offices from Road Development and Outer Ring Road responsibilities. SUDA and highway projects require separate attribution.
- Jaipur: JMC zone executive engineers are evident in current road repair tenders. Keep JDA regional infrastructure and state PWD/NHAI corridor records independently. Resolve ward renumbering using each document's date.
- Nagpur: NMC zone engineering. Historical NIT, PWD or NHAI construction does not establish the current maintainer. Reports of consolidated road maintenance need the actual handover order or asset register before changing responsibility data.
- Nashik: municipal Public Works divisions are explicitly listed in the contact directory. State PWD, NHAI, MSRDC and MIDC roles require corridor or project evidence. A municipal department called PWD is a different organization from Maharashtra State PWD.
- Navi Mumbai/Thane: NMMC/TMC municipal engineering and CIDCO, MMRDA, MIDC, MSRDC, state PWD and NHAI projects separately. Planning authority, road builder, funder and maintainer can differ. Neighbouring Panvel should have its own ULB and coverage entry.

## Recommended release gate for each city

1. Establish the current ULB boundary and its effective legal notification. Identify metropolitan and planning areas separately.
2. Obtain administrative zone or office geometry with its source version and office crosswalk. Official viewer access alone is insufficient.
3. Retain final electoral geometry as a separate versioned layer. Import old/new crosswalks where available.
4. Check polygon validity, coordinate reference system, coverage gaps/overlaps, code uniqueness and boundary-adjacent sample addresses. Keep water and non-municipal exclusions deliberate.
5. Publish navigation after geometry validation. If only the city boundary is ready, show its selector with inner detail marked pending. Do not fabricate inner zones.
6. Assign road management from asset registers, maintenance tenders, work orders, handover notifications or equivalent document evidence. Preserve owner, builder, funder, maintainer and validity dates separately.
